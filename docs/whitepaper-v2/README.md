**Version:** 2.0 (draft)  
**Status:** In progress — Part I–II drafted; Part III Ch. 10–11 drafted; MCIP-0009 (North Star) Draft  
**Target length:** 80–120 pages when complete

## One line

**Spotify optimizes for songs. MusicChain optimizes for discovery.**

---

## North Star Metric

**Successful discoveries per day** — a listener finds a new artist they had never heard before and listens for at least *X* minutes, saves them, follows them, or returns to them later.

## Relationship to v0.1

The original [WHITEPAPER.md](../../WHITEPAPER.md) (v0.1) remains a concise technical outline focused on economy and blockchain mechanics. **Whitepaper v2.0** reframes MusicChain as a discovery operating system — the narrative layer that explains *why* the technical systems exist.

| Document | Audience | Focus |
|----------|----------|-------|
| WHITEPAPER.md v0.1 | Developers, researchers | Economy, chain, MCIPs |
| WHITEPAPER v2.0 | Artists, listeners, investors, community | Discovery philosophy, network effects, growth |
