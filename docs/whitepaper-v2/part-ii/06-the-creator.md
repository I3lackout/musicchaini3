# Chapter 6 — The Creator

> *Part II — The Ecosystem*

---

## From uploader to reputation builder

Artists on MusicChain do not merely upload files. They **build reputation** in public — discovery score, authenticity, supporters, and rank. That reputation is the asset they promote when they ask fans to listen on MusicChain instead of only on incumbents.

The creator journey:

```
Upload → Early listeners (First 100) → TFPS momentum
    → Community votes → Discovery Score rises
    → Recommendations expand → Supporters grow → Creator Rank climbs
```

Each step is visible and tied to authentic engagement, not pay-for-play placement.

---

## Artist Score and Creator Rank

Artists respond to **rankings** — MusicChain should supply rankings worth sharing.

### Public artist dashboard (target)

```
Artist Score

Followers      12,400
Streams        890,000
Supporters     3,200
Creator Level  28

Creator Rank:  #183 Worldwide
```

| Metric | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Discovery Score** | How effectively new listeners find and retain this artist |
| **Authenticity Score** | Genuine engagement vs. suspicious patterns (UserChain / fraud signals) |
| **Community Trust** | Weighted reputation from listener interactions |
| **Supporters** | Followers who save, return, or allocate support McCC |
| **Live Reputation** | Performance in live and Discovery Party sessions |
| **Creator Level** | Milestone progression (Creator Journey) |


When rank is public and fresh, artists organically post:

> *"Help me reach Top 100 Rising on MusicChain!"*

That is **free marketing** aligned with discovery — artists recruit fans; fans become explorers; the [Growth Engine Phase 2](../part-vi/growth-engine-v1-spec.md) loop spins.

Rising 100 must **not** be purchasable. Selection logic belongs in an MCIP with TFPS inputs documented for transparency.

## Weekly Artist Cup

Regional competition ([Phase 3](../part-vi/growth-engine-v1-spec.md)):

```
Weekly Artist Cup — Germany

#1  Artist A
#2  Artist B
#3  Artist C
```

Streams and trust-weighted community votes count. Fans share standings; artists push for last-minute support. Competition increases **discovery events**, not empty streams — cup rules should weight first-time listeners from the region.

## First 100 as creator asset

The **First 100 Listeners** list is social proof of organic early traction. Creators can highlight:

> *"47 of my first 100 listeners still follow me."*

That narrative resists fake virality better than raw stream counts alone.

## Live feedback loop

During live sessions, artists receive **structured** community signal:

```
🔥 LIVE — 236 listeners

Current Track
Potential:       ★★★★☆
Field Pressure:  87%

Vote:  ⬆ Breakout  ·  ⬇ Needs Work
```

Immediate feedback replaces guessing whether a track “works.” TFPS incorporates live velocity where MCIP defines weights ([Chapter 11](../part-iii/11-tfps.md)).

---

## Early support (McCC) — reputation, not speculation

Listeners may allocate a **small amount of McCC** to show support ([Phase 8](../part-vi/growth-engine-v1-spec.md)):

- **Not** an investment product or return promise.
- **Supporter rank** on artist page grows with artist discovery success.
- Early supporters appear in a **Discovery circle** — status for finding artists before the crowd.

This extends the creator economy without turning MusicChain into a securities narrative.

---

## Creator economy overview

Resource flows (detail in Part IV):

```
Listen / interact → McCC, Fuel, Gas splits → Creator rewards
Sustained impact → ITC epochs (community contribution)
Support allocation → Reputation + supporter rank
```

Creators trace **how a listen became rewards** via track blockchain and MusicChain logs ([Chapter 9](./09-the-chain.md)).

---

## Creator authenticity

Verified creators, transparent moderation, and bot-resistant TFPS protect the discovery signal. Artificial engagement must not outweigh genuine listener support — core principle from [Part I](../part-i/04-vision-mission.md).

Artists who cheat rankings lose Authenticity Score and Rising 100 eligibility. The platform wins trust by **punishing manipulation visibly**, not by hiding the leaderboard.
