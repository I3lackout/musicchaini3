# Chapter 7 — The Community

> *Part II — The Ecosystem*

---

## Community as discovery engine

The community is not an audience sitting below a stage. It is a **sensor network** for discovery — voting, listening together, completing missions, and governing what gets elevated.

```
More community → More ratings → Better TFPS → Better discovery → More community
```

That is Loop 4 from [Network Effects](../part-vi/24-network-effects.md). Without community participation, MusicChain reduces to another opaque recommender. With it, hidden gems surface because **people** decide, assisted by AI — not replaced.

---

## Discovery Parties

One of the strongest community formats for MusicChain:

```
🎧 Electronic Discovery Room

50 listeners · unknown artists emphasis

Now playing: [Artist — Track]

👍   ❤️   🔥   👎
```

**Discover together:** everyone hears the same candidate tracks; votes aggregate into TFPS and party playlists. Gems rise because the room validated them — not because a single user skipped past in three seconds.

Discovery Parties connect:

- [Live Infrastructure](../part-v/23-live-infrastructure.md) (real-time audio)
- [TFPS](../part-iii/11-tfps.md) (field pressure from vote bursts)
- [Growth Engine Phase 5](../part-vi/growth-engine-v1-spec.md)

Hosts earn **Community XP**; participants earn **Discovery XP** when they save or follow from a party context.

## Community missions

Cooperative goals replace vague “listen more” prompts:

```
Mission: Germany needs 1,000,000 listening minutes this week.

Progress: ████████░░  820,000 / 1,000,000

On completion: +20 Fuel (eligible contributors)
```

Listening becomes **cooperative**. Missions should prefer **new-artist minutes** or **successful discoveries** where possible so missions serve the [Music Discovery Law](../part-i/03-music-discovery-law.md), not passive replay farming.

Eligibility: minimum contribution threshold; UserChain trust gate for payout.

## Today’s Hidden Gem (community FOMO)

Daily spotlight with countdown:

```
Today's Hidden Gem
Available for: 23:59:44
```

Tomorrow’s gem replaces today’s — encourages daily return. Selection: TFPS rise + community shortlist or editorial transparency note. **Not sold** to highest bidder.

---

## Moderation and trust

Community reporting feeds **UserChain** trust scores. High-trust votes weigh more in TFPS and party outcomes; low-trust accounts still participate but do not dominate rankings.

Transparent moderation principles are in [Chapter 13](../part-iii/13-trust-economy.md) and [Security](../part-v/21-security.md). Fake virality is a community threat, not only a platform threat.

---

## Social discovery

- **Friend shares** — mentor XP when share converts to discovery
- **Collaborative playlists** — post-party and mission outputs
- **Referral codes** — Phase 1 viral loop ([Growth Engine](../part-vi/25-growth-engine.md))

Discovery that starts in Discord or Instagram still counts when it brings **authentic listeners** onto MusicChain.

## The community and the Music Discovery Law

| Community action | Discovery effect |
|------------------|------------------|
| Discovery Party vote | Elevates validated unknowns |
| Mission completion | Coordinated exploration at scale |
| Prediction game | Trains breakout signal |

The community answers: **Who deserves to be discovered next?** — together.
