# Chapter 8 — The AI

> *Part II — The Ecosystem* · **Status: skeleton**

---

## Overview

AI assists discovery — it does not replace human taste. This chapter defines the boundary between machine pattern recognition and human decision-making.

## Planned sections

### 8.1 Discovery Intelligence

```
Human Discovery
       +
AI Pattern Recognition
       ↓
Discovery Intelligence
```

### 8.2 What AI does

- Identifies promising music patterns in TFPS data
- Surfaces candidates for explorer profiles (Discovery DNA matching)
- Assists moderation and fraud detection
- Provides creator insights and trend prediction
- Optimizes discovery pipeline (not retention pipeline)

### 8.3 What AI does not do

- Replace human listening decisions
- Optimize for engagement addiction
- Operate as a black box without explainability
- Promote music based on payment rather than signal

### 8.4 AI and the Music Discovery Law

Every AI model must pass: *Does this increase the probability that the next great artist is discovered?*
